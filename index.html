A note on the kitchen table

Glad you found us.

Cherry Flats is still the name on the map for our stretch of Horse Creek, after the wild cherries that grew here once. The cherries are gone. What's here now is chickens, a garden that gets away from me every August, a sourdough starter with more personality than some people, and two boys who can do more than you'd think.

This is where I share what we make, what we're learning, and the books we love. Take your time. There's always another chair.

Rachel
